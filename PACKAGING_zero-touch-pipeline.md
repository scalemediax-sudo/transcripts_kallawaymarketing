## ⚠️ STRATEGY CONFLICT — resolve before publishing

Kallaway standing notes for this account define the audience as *"young people learning AI who want
to build an agency. NOT business owners."* This repackage targets owners.

Consequences of the switch:
- **TAM shrinks.** Owner-targeted titles get lower CTR than builder titles. Fewer, better clicks.
- **Follows/video will drop** — the metric the account is supposed to be measured on.
- **Calls booked becomes the metric instead.** If that's the trade, make it deliberately.

Recommendation: run this as a **deliberate test**, not a permanent shift. One owner-packaged video,
measure calls booked, then decide.

## THE IDEA

### Contrarian take (one sentence)
Business owners think they have a marketing problem, but they have a **prospecting** problem —
the customers are already listed publicly, nobody is going and getting them.

### Villain
Manual prospecting. Copying names off Google Maps into a sheet. Paying a lead-list vendor.
Hiring an SDR to do a job that a script does better.

### Term brand
> **The Zero-Touch Pipeline**

Names the *mechanism*, not the tool — which is the whole point. Owners don't care that it's
Claude Code. They care that nobody touches it.

Alternates: *The Silent Pipeline · Zero-Touch Prospecting · The Ghost Rep*

### One Degree framing
Never say "money" or "revenue." Say **customers · pipeline · your calendar · the part that pays.**
"Customers" is the correct one-degree noun for this avatar.

### Evidence (Lawyer Method) — Dhruvesh must supply real numbers
- [N] leads pulled in the last 7 days
- [N] emails sent, [N] replies, [N] calls booked
- Cost comparison: $[N]/mo lead tool → $20 Claude plan
- Screen recording of the dashboard filling — this is the bullseye proof

⚠️ **Do not invent these.** Every `[N]` must be a real figure or the Specificity Effect backfires
into a bait-and-switch signal.

### Shock score (of 100 owners, how many are shocked?)

|---|---|---|

## THUMBNAIL — the text hook

**This is the documented biggest gap on this account. Specify it every time.**

The title carries the **claim**. The thumbnail carries the **proof**. Never the same words twice.

### Pairing A — with title #3 (recommended)
- **TEXT (≤3 words):** `ZERO-TOUCH`  — with a small kicker: *[N] customers*
- **VISUAL:** Split frame. Left — Dhruvesh, hands visibly up and off the keyboard, calm, not
  surprised. Right — a real inbox showing reply threads, or the dashboard mid-fill.
- **Stun-gun category:** contradiction (hands off + results on screen).

### Pairing B — with title #6
- **TEXT:** `[N] CUSTOMERS` (large) / `0 CLICKS` (small, beneath)
- **VISUAL:** Google Maps greyed out or slashed on the left; a full booked calendar on the right.
- Carries the topic context that title #6 lacks.

### Pairing C — with title #9
- **TEXT:** `THE PIPELINE`
- **VISUAL:** Simple flow — map pin → contact rows → inbox. Warm colours, business register.

### Hard rules
- **No terminal, no code, no VS Code visible.** That single visual cue re-sorts the audience
  back to builders and undoes the entire repackage.
- **No "FREE".**
- Dhruvesh's expression: calm authority. Not wide-eyed. Shock faces read as tutorial-bait.
- **Spook test:** Kallaway — viewers click a promised solve *unless the thumbnail makes them
  suspect a bait-and-switch*, and that suspicion comes from unclear or low-quality design.
  Use a real screenshot, never a mocked-up one. Legible at phone size. 3 elements maximum.

## FIRST 2 MINUTES — the actual bottleneck

AVD is 19.5% against a 20.6% baseline. Kallaway's rule: **spend 50%+ of total effort on idea,
title, packaging and the first two minutes.** Currently the first two minutes are the weakest part.

**Problem 1 — trust anchor is the wrong rung.**
Current: *"I'm Dhruvesh, founder of Video AI, we grew 10,000 followers on Instagram."*
For an owner avatar that's rung 4 (association) and lands at ~0:50, right at the drop-off.
Instagram follower counts do not make a business owner trust you with their pipeline.

**Fix:** rung 5 — direct results from this exact system.
> "This system booked me [N] calls last month. I have not opened Google Maps once."

Move it to **line 2**, not 0:50.

**Problem 2 — no second hook by 0:15.** Add the reveal early: show the dashboard already full of
leads before explaining anything. Open at 70/100, spike to 90 by second 10.

**Problem 3 — the IDE explainer at 1:00–2:18** (Claude Code app vs IDE, token efficiency) is
pure builder content sitting inside the retention danger zone. For an owner cut it entirely or
push it past 6:00. It is the single most likely churn point.

**Problem 4 — connect beats with BUT / THEREFORE, never "and then."**
